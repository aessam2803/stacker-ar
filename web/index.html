Machine cycle (28 s loop)

  1. Wait – cradle at load height; IR sensor watches the load level.
  2. Load – a 500-sheet A4 ream is placed; the IR sensor detects it.
  3. Index – stepper turns the lead screw; cradle drops 52 mm (one ream).
  4. Repeat until 5 reams are stacked.
  5. Hand-over – cradle lowers below the belt, so the stack rests on the belt.
  6. Transfer – belt moves the whole stack onto the outfeed rollers.
  7. Reset – cradle returns to load height.
